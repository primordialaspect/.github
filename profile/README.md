## Primordial Aspect

A research lab working on thirty years of public data.

We build deep databases on companies, and the people and networks behind them, from public sources only: registries, filings, court records, press and archives across the EU, the UK and parts of the US. Each database can be queried directly from your own AI assistant.

**For**
- **Journalists**: a complete, sourced picture of a company, ready to question.
- **Investors and dealmakers**: every public layer on a company in one place, before a decision.
